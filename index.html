About

I am currently completing my MSc in Computer Science at the Université du Québec à Chicoutimi (UQAC), Québec, Canada. My research sits at the intersection of trustworthy AI, software verification and testing.

I hold a Technical University Diploma in Computer Engineering and a Bachelor in Software Engineering from the Institut Universitaire de Technologie (IUT) Ngaoundéré (Cameroon), and a first MSc in Computer Engineering from the Pan African University Institute for Basic Science, Technology, and Innovation (PAUSTI), Nairobi, Kenya. During my studies I have completed multiple internships, published research papers, and built modern and responsive web applications, and AI/ML models.

I am open to Sofware developer, AI/ML engineering and research opportunities in Canada.
